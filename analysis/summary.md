## Method

- Daily bars only; no intraday or opening-range logic. 20/50-day simple MAs, 14-day ATR (Wilder), swing points = 5-bar pivots (a high / low with 5 lower / higher bars each side).
- **Stop (long):** the tighter of (a) the higher of the latest swing low and the 20-day MA, minus 0.25 ATR, taken only if it is still below price, and (b) entry - 1.5 ATR. Shorts mirror it above. A winner's stop therefore trails above entry; a loser more than 1.5 ATR through entry is past stop.
- **R:** entry-to-stop distance while the stop is at least 0.5 ATR on the risk side of entry; once it has trailed to break-even or through entry, R is the 1.5 ATR risk unit. R-multiple = open move / R.
- **Target (long):** nearest prior swing high above both entry and price, else entry + 2R (shorts: nearest prior swing low below both, else entry - 2R). When price is already through 2R with no swing level left beyond it, the 2R level stays on the chart as reached and the position is managed by its trailing stop.
- **Stops are evaluated as of today.** Entry dates are not in the book, so the stop is today's structure and today's ATR, not the stop that applied on the day each position was opened.
- **Status** (first that applies): past stop; near stop (within 0.5 ATR); near target (within 0.5 ATR); winner / trail (R >= 1 or through target); flat.
- **Ticker mapping:** Bloomberg suffix -> Yahoo suffix; 3081 TT trades on TPEx (3081.TWO); PNG CN on the TSX-V; MAXQ CN on Cboe Canada (.NE); Swedish B shares use a dash (KEBNI-B.ST, SECT-B.ST). HY9H GR: levels are computed on SK Hynix's Korean line 000660.KS and converted to the GDR's EUR price (HY9H.F) at the latest close ratio, so entry, current, stop and target are all in EUR; its chart is in KRW with the entry converted the same way.
